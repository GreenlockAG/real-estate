TOP 5 RISK METRICS FOR PRIVATE WEALTH ( $ 50M+)
Selection Criteria: Governance + Behavioral Realism + Actionability
Private wealth is NOT an endowment or pension. Key differences:

Principal psychology: Family members see marks monthly/quarterly and react emotionally
Liquidity constraints: Can't always rebalance optimally; capital calls create forced timing
Governance simplicity: No 40-person risk committee; decisions made by 1-3 people
Longevity risk: Multi-generational; must survive 50+ years of regime shifts
Spending inflexibility: Can't cut distributions 30% like an endowment can


THE TOP 5 METRICS (Ranked by Decision Impact)
#1: MAX DRAWDOWN (DD) — "The Panic Threshold"
What: Peak-to-trough portfolio lossWhy It's #1 for Private Wealth:

Behavioral anchor: Families fire advisors/abandon plans based on DD, not ES or Sharpe
Regime-agnostic: Works across bull/bear/inflation cycles
Governance clarity: "We size the portfolio so DD stays under 25%" is understandable

#2: LIQUIDITY COVERAGE RATIO (LCR) — "Can We Survive Without Forced Sales?"
What: Available liquidity ÷ Next 12-24 months net cash needs (calls + distributions + expenses)Why It's #2:

Death spiral prevention: Prevents selling Growth/Hedges at worst possible time
Private asset enabler: Lets you hold illiquid positions through cycles
Behaviorally stabilizing: Knowing "we have 18 months of liquidity" reduces panic

How to Calculate:
LCR = (T-bills + Short IG + Available credit lines) 
      ÷ (Next 12mo capital calls + distributions + expenses - expected distributions from PE/RE)

Target: ≥ 1.5x (conservative); ≥ 1.25x (moderate)
Stress Test:

Scenario 1: All unfunded PE calls + no distributions for 12 months
Scenario 2: Above + unexpected  $ 5M principal need (medical, divorce, investment opportunity)

#4: EXPECTED SHORTFALL (ES95 or ES97.5) — "Average Loss in Bad Years"
What: Mean return in worst 5% (or 2.5%) of Monte Carlo outcomesWhy It's #4 (Not #1):

Better than VaR: Captures tail severity, not just threshold
Optimizer-friendly: Can minimize ES in portfolio construction
Complements DD: DD is "worst single path"; ES is "average of worst paths"

#5: CORRELATION STRESS INDEX (CSI) — "Will My Hedges Work When I Need Them?"
What: Rolling 12-month correlation between Growth sleeve and Hedges sleeveWhy It's #5:

Early warning system: Detects when diversification is breaking down
Hedge effectiveness: Validates that duration/TIPS/CTA are still negatively correlated to equities
Actionable: Triggers tactical shifts in Hedges sleeve composition

How to Calculate:
CSI = Rolling 12-month correlation (Growth sleeve returns, Hedges sleeve returns)

Target: CSI < -0.3 (strong negative); Warning: CSI > -0.1
